November 2026: When to Sell or Trade Your Pokémon Collection

By Elburn Card Shop · November 3, 2026 · 6 min read

If you've been sitting on a Pokemon collection—sealed boxes, graded slabs, bulk singles—November is your window. The market is calm. Prices are fair. Holiday buyers are just waking up. And if you're planning to move inventory before year-end, right now is when smart collectors act.

Here's the November selling and trading playbook for every type of Pokemon collection.

Why November Is Prime Selling Season

The 30th Celebration set shipped heavily through September and October. Hype has cooled. Delta Reign (Mega Rayquaza) launches November 6 with prerelease events already closed. This creates a rare market moment:

  • No New Hype Distractions: Summer's chaos has passed. Collectors are in evaluation mode, not panic-buying.
  • Holiday Budget Season Starts: December spending begins now. Buyers are actively shopping for gift-quality product and graded slabs.
  • Reprints Have Stabilized: If you're holding reprint stock from earlier this year, November pricing is more predictable than October volatility.
  • Grading Queues Open Up: PSA and BGS queues ease after the 30th-anniversary crush. If you need cards graded before selling, now's the time.
💡 Smart Collector Insight: December is when family members, corporate buyers, and casual collectors flood the market with urgency. They're buying at any price. If you want fair value, sell before then. If you want peak price, hold for December—but you risk getting stuck with overstocked inventory in January.

Selling Strategy by Collection Type

Sealed Product (Boxes, ETBs, Bundles)

Sealed Pokemon boxes are stable in November. Stellar Crown and Surging Sparks are proven sets with 2+ months of price history. If you bought these at MSRP and want to move them now, you're looking at fair-market pricing with no downside surprise.

Selling tip: Local card shops (especially Elburn Card Shop near Geneva IL) will inspect sealed product for corner damage, shelf wear, and authenticity before making an offer. This transparency beats shipping the box to an online retailer. You walk away with cash same-day. Online consignment takes 60+ days and eats 20-25% commission.

Holding cost: zero. Selling cost: 5-10% markdown from market (fair for local liquidity).

Graded Slabs (PSA, BGS, CGC)

November is ideal for graded card sales. Holiday gift buyers are shopping for museum-quality slabs. A PSA 8 or PSA 9 classic Pokemon card is displayable, investable, and holds value.

Local card shops with graded inventory can move these fast. Online platforms (eBay, TCGPlayer) take 10-15% in fees plus shipping risk. Why lose that margin?

Pricing tip: Graded cards at local shops sell at 5-15% market discount for speed. You get paid now. Online buyers get a deal. Everyone wins. If you're sitting on high-grade slabs (PSA 9, PSA 10), hold through December—holiday collectors pay a premium for trophy cards.

Bulk Singles & Mixed Collections

Large lots of ungraded singles can move at local card shops much faster than online. Dealers buy bulk at 30-50% of TCGPlayer value, but you get instant liquidity. No shipping, no eBay fees, no listing headaches.

Selling strategy: Separate your collection into three tiers:

  • Tier 1 (Diamonds): High-value singles (Charizard ex, Rayquaza ex, investment cards). Sell these individually at a local shop or online where they command premium pricing.
  • Tier 2 (Playables): Staple tournament cards. Move these to a shop—dealers buy playables at 60-70% of market value because they turn fast.
  • Tier 3 (Commons/Bulk): Everything else. Bulk lots move at 25-40% of book value, but again: instant cash, zero shipping cost, zero time investment.

What NOT to Do This November

  • Don't wait for December: Yes, holiday gift buyers exist. But so does year-end tax loss harvesting and impulse dumping. Sell early, not late.
  • Don't ship valuable cards to online buyers: Loss-in-transit claims, buyer disputes, and PayPal chargebacks eat your margin. Local = risk-free.
  • Don't overstuff your asking price: If you're selling to a dealer, understand they need 15-25% margin to flip the card or resell the lot. Price accordingly and deals close.
  • Don't hold junk expecting miracles: Common cards from reprinted sets aren't going to moon in value. Sell the bulk now at fair price. Holding cost is real.

The November Buying Guide (If You're Upgrading)

Not every collector is selling. Some are trading up—moving older collection to capture Delta Reign boxes or graded upgrades. Here's the November buying angle:

  • Reprints Are Cheap: Summer reprints from May-June are 40-60% off MSRP by November. If you wanted Stellar Crown cheap, today is the day.
  • Sealed Product Is Deep: Shops have full stock before December rush. Selection will be picked over by month-end.
