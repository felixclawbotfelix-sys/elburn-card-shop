The Mid-November Buying Window: Why Now is the Best Time to Buy Pokemon Cards

By Elburn Card Shop · November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Delta Reign dropped 10 days ago. The hype is real, but the prices are cooling. Right now — mid-November — is one of the best buying windows of the entire year. If you've been waiting to buy sealed product, today is the day to act.

Here's why, and what to buy before Thanksgiving buyers push prices back up.

The Price Drop Pattern: What Always Happens After Launch

Every Pokemon TCG set follows the same pattern. Preorder prices spike. Launch day comes with a small dip. Then, 7-14 days after release, prices collapse 15-25% as the initial speculators bail and the market settles.

Delta Reign launched November 6. It's now November 16. You're in the sweet spot—prices have dropped from launch highs but haven't yet recovered for the holiday buying surge that starts the week of Thanksgiving.

If you buy this week, you're buying at a discount before holiday demand kicks in. If you wait until November 22, you'll pay 10-15% more because families are gift shopping and prices rise with demand.

What This Means for Sealed Product

Booster boxes are the clearest example. Delta Reign boxes at preorder were $130-140. At launch, they dropped to $110-120. Right now, mid-November, they're $95-105 at most retailers. That's a 25-30% drop from preorder.

Elite Trainer Boxes, 30th Celebration Premium Collections, and specialty sets all follow the same curve. November 16 is when retail prices are at their lowest before holiday inflation.

The exception: Japanese imports. Those move even faster—prices drop within days and bottom out in the first two weeks. If you're chasing Japanese Mega Era boxes, they're at their November lows right now.

Why Not Wait Longer?

You might think: if hype is cooling, won't prices go lower? Not necessarily. Here's what happens next:

  • Thanksgiving (Nov 22-28): Holiday gift buying begins. Prices start rising as family members buy for collectors. Retail stock starts moving.
  • December: Prices stabilize around current retail, then creep up as holiday shopping accelerates and stock dwindles.
  • January onward: Prices hold or go up as the set ages out of "new release" status and becomes scarce.

The lowest prices for Delta Reign will be this week. After Thanksgiving, they'll never be this cheap again until the next set launches in February.

🎯 Pro Move: Buy Delta Reign sealed now, hold through the holidays, and sell in January when holiday buyers realize they overpaid. That's a clean 15-20% arbitrage play using mid-November timing.

What to Buy Right Now

  • Delta Reign Booster Boxes (if holding as investment): $95-105. Buy, hold sealed, sell post-holidays.
  • 30th Celebration Premium Collections & Figure Collections: These hold collector demand year-round. Lock in mid-November prices before holiday demand hits.
  • Earlier 2026 Sets (Chaos Rising, Pitch Black): These are already 2-3 months old and deeply discounted. Perfect for collectors who want volume without chase-set prices.
  • Japanese Mega Era Sealed (M2a, M3, M4, M5): Japanese boxes bottom out first and fastest. November 16 is likely their lowest price point for the year.

What NOT to Buy Right Now

Avoid speculating on loose booster packs or bulk singles right now. The card meta is still settling, and you'll get better prices on singles in December once the competitive format stabilizes.
