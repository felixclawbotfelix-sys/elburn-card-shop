Chase Cards

Faker on-card auto or Masterpiece Patch — these are the top hits in the box. Faker is arguably the most recognizable name in esports globally. Any low-numbered Faker signature card commands serious collector attention.

The Team Autograph — signed by the entire roster — is also a massive card. Six signatures from players of this caliber on a single card is rare regardless of sport or game.

Jersey and Name Patch relic cards are the mid-tier hits. Like K-pop relics, these contain actual material from worn uniforms.
