Rookie Cards Worth Watching Heading Into the 2025–26 Season

By Elburn Card Shop · September 2026 · 5 min read

Every year the sports card market resets with a new class of rookies — and every year there are a handful of names that collectors wish they'd grabbed early. We've put together our picks for the 2025–26 season across football, basketball, and baseball. We carry Panini and Topps products in-store, so if you're looking to pull these yourself, come see us.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Card collecting involves market risk. Values fluctuate based on player performance, market demand, and supply. This is our take as hobbyists — not financial advice. Only spend what you're comfortable losing.

🏈 Football — 2025 NFL Draft Class

The 2025 NFL Draft was loaded at the top, and several players are already generating buzz in the card market before the regular season even kicks off. Key sets to watch: 2024 Panini Select Football and 2024 Panini Donruss Optic Football — both available at Elburn Card Shop.

🏈 Watch List

Quarterback Prospects

First-round quarterbacks are always the highest-upside rookie cards. Look for autographed rookies in Prizm and Select — these hold the most long-term value if the player pans out. Chrome-based cards (Prizm, Optic) photograph and grade the best.

💡 Strategy tip: In football, the safest rookie cards are quarterbacks taken in the top 5 picks who land in a good situation — a winning team, a good offensive line, and an experienced coaching staff. Check the landing spot before you buy.

🏀 Basketball — 2025–26 NBA Season

Basketball is one of the hottest segments of the card market right now. The 2024–25 Panini Prizm Basketball and 2025 NBA Hoops sets are both in-store and are your best bet for pulling key rookies from the latest class.

🏀 Watch List

Top Lottery Picks

Top-5 NBA draft picks in the first two years of their career represent the highest upside in basketball. Rookie Patch Autos (RPAs) from Prizm are the gold standard — they typically 3–10x if the player becomes an All-Star. Focus on players who showed NBA-ready skills in college or overseas.

🏀 Budget Pick

Retail Blaster Strategy

The 2025 NBA Hoops Retail Blaster (90 cards) we carry is a great entry-level way to chase rookies. Lower hit rates than hobby boxes, but at a fraction of the price — good for collectors who want to hunt without a big upfront investment.

⚾ Baseball — 2025 Topps Update

The 2025 Topps Update Series Baseball is the set to watch for mid-season call-ups and second-half additions. Update is historically where some of the most valuable rookie cards live — players called up after the base set is printed get their first Topps cards here.

⚾ Watch List

Mid-Season Call-Ups

Update Series rookies have historically been very valuable — this is where many of the most iconic Topps rookie cards originated. Look for players making their MLB debut in the second half of 2025. Chrome refractors and autographed rookies are the premium targets.

💡 Baseball tip: In baseball, pitchers are riskier cards than position players — injury risk is much higher. Focus on outfielders and catchers who showed big minor league numbers. Prospect rankings from Baseball America and FanGraphs are your friends.

General Strategy for Rookie Cards

Regardless of sport, here's how to approach rookie card collecting smart:

  1. Buy early. The best time to get a rookie card is before performance drives the price. Pre-season and early in Year 1 are typically the best windows.
  2. Focus on chrome-based cards. Prizm, Optic, and Chrome-branded cards photograph better, grade better, and hold value better than base paper cards.
  3. Prioritize autographed cards. Certified autos (not sticker autos when possible) carry the highest premiums and age the best.
  4. Know when to sell. If a player wins Rookie of the Year or makes an All-Star team, that's often the peak. Be willing to take profits.
  5. Don't over-invest in one player. Spread across multiple rookies — most won't pan out, but the ones who do will more than make up for the misses.
