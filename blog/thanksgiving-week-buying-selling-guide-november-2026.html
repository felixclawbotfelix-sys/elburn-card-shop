Thanksgiving Week Card Deals: What to Buy & Sell Before Year-End

By Elburn Card Shop · November 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Thanksgiving week is live. Family gatherings are ramping up, holiday shopping has officially begun, and the card market is in a strange moment: prices are cooling on some products while heating up on others. If you're a collector, investor, or just gift shopping for a card enthusiast, here's exactly what moves to make right now before year-end.

The Thanksgiving Week Market Shift

Three weeks ago (early November), prices were climbing hard on new releases. Delta Reign hit November 6 with excitement, and prices spiked. But we're now 17 days post-launch. The retail price surge has peaked, and two things are happening:

  • Sealed product is stabilizing. Booster boxes, Elite Trainer Boxes, and specialty collections have stopped climbing. They're holding around $95-110 for Delta Reign boxes.
  • Older sets are getting cheaper. Chaos Rising, Perfect Order, and earlier 2026 releases are being discounted as retailers make room for holiday inventory.

This creates a window. You have about 48 hours (through Thanksgiving) to lock in deals before the final pre-Christmas buying surge begins the week of November 24-30.

What to Buy Right Now (Nov 23-25)

This is your last 48-hour window for the best prices before the final holiday push:

  • Earlier 2026 Sets (Chaos Rising, Pitch Black): These are sitting in inventory everywhere because Delta Reign is the new hotness. Buy now at 20-30% off MSRP. By December 10, these will be harder to find and prices will creep up. Hold through the holidays and sell in January.
  • Perfect Order ETBs & Booster Bundles: Perfect Order is a solid mid-range set with consistent collector demand. ETBs are sitting around $35-40 this week. After Thanksgiving, they'll be $45-50 as family gift-givers realize they need "something for the card collector." Lock in now.
  • Japanese MEGA Boxes (M2a, M3, M4, M5): Japanese sealed inventory is tight and getting tighter. If you've been thinking about collecting Japanese MEGA era, this is the last week prices are reasonable before holiday collectors realize they exist. December prices will jump 20-30%.
  • One Piece TCG Booster Boxes: One Piece is building momentum but still under the radar of most holiday gift-givers. Supply is good, prices are stable. Buy now, hold for 4 weeks, sell mid-December when gifting picks up and stock starts to drop.
  • Sports Cards (Panini, Topps): Baseball, basketball, and football card stock is plentiful right now. 2024-25 Panini Prizm Basketball is a solid long-term hold, and prices are stable going into Thanksgiving. Grab what you need this week before gifting season accelerates.
⚡ Thanksgiving Arbitrage Play: Buy Perfect Order and older 2026 sets at Thanksgiving prices (10-15% under retail). Hold sealed for 3 weeks. Sell mid-December to holiday-delayed gift-givers at full retail or above. That's a clean 15-25% gain in 3 weeks with zero risk.

What to Sell This Week (Nov 23-30)

If you're sitting on inventory, Thanksgiving week is a solid selling window—but don't wait past November 30:

  • Graded Slabs (PSA 9/10): Thanksgiving is when collectors with disposable holiday cash are online shopping for luxury pieces. Prices for graded cards are stable and fair. If you have high-grade slabs, this is a good week to list them before December shipping delays hit.
  • Loose Booster Packs (Any Set): People gift booster packs, and they're buying them right now. Supply is high everywhere, so price aggressively. Volume over margin this week—push packs at MSRP or below to move inventory. December is too late; everyone will have gift inventory filled.
  • Hobby Boxes from Summer 2026: If you're holding May/June/July set booster boxes, sell them now. These are old news and prices have compressed. Thanksgiving is your last good window before they become worthless. Liquidate at any margin above cost.
  • Singles from Older Sets: Bulk singles from 2025 and earlier are good to sell this week. Holiday gift-givers often pick up "5 random cool cards" for kids' stocking stuffers. The volume will be there.

What NOT to Touch This Week

Avoid these moves during Thanksgiving week:

  • Don't buy Delta Reign at market price. It's too recent and prices are still settling. Wait until December 15+ when it's 3 weeks old and prices have stabilized further.
  • Don't hold onto summer inventory. Old stock is getting expensive to warehouse. Move it this week at any margin.
  • Don't speculate on one-off expensive singles. Holiday gift-givers are buying sealed, not chasing chase cards. Singles will be cheaper in January after people open their holiday gifts and hit the market.

The Next Phase: December 1-15

Once December 1 hits, the market changes again. Here's what happens:

  • Sealed prices start rising as holiday supply tightens
  • Gift-givers panic-buy anything that's left in stock
  • Shipping delays begin (5-7 days becomes 2+ weeks)
  • Retail prices start climbing toward year-end premiums

If you're buying, finish this week. If you're selling, start now and push harder through Nov 30. December will be frantic and expensive.
