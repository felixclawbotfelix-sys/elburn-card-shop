2. Legendary Stadium Mechanic (New): Delta Reign introduces the Legendary Stadium pair mechanic — two Stadiums can be active simultaneously, opening deck-building space for Mega strategies that didn't exist before. Competitive viability creates casual-to-competitive spillover demand.

3. 30th Anniversary Nostalgia: 2026 marks 30 years of Pokémon. Collectors are revisiting favorite eras. Many loved Mega Evolution during its original run (2014-2016). Anniversaries unlock emotional attachment and buying momentum.
