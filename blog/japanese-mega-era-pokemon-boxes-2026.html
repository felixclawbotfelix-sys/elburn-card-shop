Japanese MEGA-Era Pokémon Boxes: M2a, M3, M4 & M5

Four sealed Japanese MEGA-era Pokémon boxes just landed at Elburn Card Shop. All carry The Pokémon Company's official 4521329 barcode prefix, Japan-only labeling, and factory shrink wrap. Here's everything you need to know about each one.

Quick comparison: If you're only buying one, M2a MEGA Dream ex is the standout — guaranteed hit in every pack and the deepest chase list. Nihil Zero and Abyss Eye are the most affordable and work great as break boxes.

Yes. All four carry The Pokémon Company's official 4521329 barcode prefix, Japan-only labeling, and factory shrink wrap. These are authentic Japanese Pokémon TCG products.
