About Chinese Pokémon TCG

What makes Simplified Chinese cards unique

🌏 Exclusive to China

Simplified Chinese Pokémon cards are officially licensed and printed exclusively for the mainland China market. They are not available through standard English or Japanese distribution channels, making them rare and collectible outside of China.

💎 Gem Pack Series

The Gem Pack (宝石包) series is a unique Chinese-exclusive product line featuring themed booster packs and promotional sets. Each volume spotlights a different Pokémon with exclusive artwork and card designs specific to the Chinese market.

🎴 Are They Legal?

Chinese Pokémon TCG cards are official Pokémon products licensed by The Pokémon Company International. They are legal for play in many formats and highly collectible. Cards use the same game mechanics as English versions.

📦 Factory Sealed

All Gem Packs in stock are factory sealed — untouched since production. Great for collectors who want to preserve the product, or for opening to chase the unique Chinese-exclusive pulls inside.
